Why Current Solutions Fail

Traditional warning signs are static and easy to ignore. By the time drivers see an animal, it's often too late.

  • ❌ Static signs don't indicate real-time danger
  • ❌ No alerts sent to approaching drivers
  • ❌ No active deterrent to move animals away
  • ❌ Night visibility extremely poor
